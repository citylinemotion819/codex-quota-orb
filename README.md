## 🌐 How to Add Your First RSS Feed

Now that omafeed is running, it's time to bring in some content. Here's a simple walkthrough:

1.  **Find the "Add Feed" button:** Look for a plus sign (+) or a button that says "Add Feed" or "Subscribe." It's usually in the top menu or toolbar.
2.  **Paste the feed URL:** When you click that button, a small window will appear. This is where you'll paste the address of an RSS feed. But where do you find an RSS feed address? Here are some tips:
    - **Look for the RSS icon:** Many websites display an orange square with white radio waves – that's the universal RSS symbol. Right-click it and select "Copy Link Address."
    - **Check the website's URL:** For many sites, you can simply add "/feed" to the end of the main website address. For example, `example.com` becomes `example.com/feed`.
    - **Browse a directory:** Some websites have a dedicated page listing all their available feeds. Look for a link like "Feeds" or "Subscribe" in the footer.
3.  **Confirm and enjoy:** Paste the copied address into omafeed's "Add Feed" box and press "Add" or "Subscribe." The feed will instantly appear in your list, and omafeed will fetch the latest articles.
